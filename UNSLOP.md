# Unslop pass

Done with the unslop skill (unslop by Damir Sokolovsky, @heydamir, CC BY 4.0) on November 24, 2026.

## Audit

Light slop: a well set sheet with good type and a real print layout, plus a handful of tells.

- Dotted pattern washed over the backdrop: `body` in `styles.css`
- Two-tone accent bar across the top of the sheet: `.sheet::before`
- Monogram in a tinted circle, set in italic: `.monogram`
- Italic role line and italic company names: `.role`, `.timeline h3 span`
- Uppercase, widely tracked 700 section labels and uppercase dates: `h2`, `.when`
- Skills as tinted pills: `.tags li`
- Projects as three equal boxed cards: `.projects li`
- Pill buttons that lift on hover: `.btn`
- A tinted, wide drop shadow under the sheet; 600 weights on titles and labels

## Direction

- Product: the resume of Nadia Rhee, a senior product manager in Chicago, read by hiring managers on screen and on paper
- Character: composed, factual, quietly sure
- World: a CV on good letter stock on a hiring manager's desk, black ink and one teal pen
- Moments: the experience section, where outcomes carry the page; the sheet itself on the desk
- Signature: dates hanging in a teal left margin, like a ledger the eye runs down, on screen and in print

## Passes

- Spacing: hanging date column (5.25rem plus a 1rem gap), project notes aligned to the same text column
- Color: dotted wash and top bar removed; teal kept for section labels, dates, bullet marks and links; neutral shadow
- Icons: toolbar icons at stroke 1.5
- Copy: unchanged (already plain and specific)
- Type: no italic, sentence case labels at 500, titles at 500, dates in tabular figures
- Layout: same sections and order; projects as a plain list under the experience column
- Details: buttons at 8px radius; skills as plain text; monogram as a hairline circle in ink
- Visuals: none added
- Motion: hover lift removed; color change on hover and a small press scale, one ease-out curve

`resume.pdf` was regenerated from the new print styles (one US Letter page).
